Change Ringing Corpus · 2012–2024 · Four sources, one schema

Every reported performance,
every method, every bell.
In one queryable record.

Change ringing has kept meticulous records for three centuries, in four separate collections that have never been joined. This corpus joins them, checks every year against an independent count, and publishes what the joined data will and will not support.

What is in it

Performances and their bands from BellBoard; towers, bells and founders from Dove’s Guide; methods and their place notation from the CCCBR Methods Library; compositions from CompLib. The four are normalised into one relational schema, so a question can cross between them — which methods are rung on bells cast by which foundry, in which counties, on which nights of the week.

Every year of the performance record is checked against BellBoard’s own independent count before it is published, and the linkage rates, the classifier accuracy and the parts that could not be matched are all stated rather than rounded away. Several of the findings below overturn what ringers generally believe; two of them are negative results, published because a checked negative is worth more than an unchecked positive.

The pages

Each one asks a question the joined corpus can answer, and says how far the answer can be trusted.
