The Change Ringing Corpus

Thirteen years of performances, resolved into a single relational dataset.

A Complete Record

Change ringing is a highly structured mathematical performance art. For over three centuries, ringers have developed thousands of "methods" — complex permutations of ringing sequences — and maintained meticulous records of their performances.

This site explores a unified corpus built from three primary sources: BellBoard’s performance archive, Dove’s Guide for Church Bell Ringers, and the CCCBR Methods Library. By normalising and linking these distinct datasets, we can visualise the entire ringing ecosystem: from the lineage of method invention and the geographic spread of foundries, to the tightly connected social networks of the ringers themselves.
