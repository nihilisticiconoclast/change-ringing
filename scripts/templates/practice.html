1. The Two Confounds in the Measurement

Comparing raw "busiest days" fails because ringing has two distinct modes: Sunday services and routine weekday practice sessions.

The Sunday Service Confound

Sunday service ringing accounts for the vast majority of short performances (quarter peals and touches) across almost every active tower. Comparing outright busiest days yields an artificial agreement rate below 16% simply because Sunday overwhelms the schedule everywhere. Excluding Sunday isolates the real weekday rhythm.

The Quarter Peal Proxy

BellBoard records published performances, not routine weekly practices. A — non-Sunday match rate (and — weekday match) measures where bands assemble to score quarter peals on practice night. It is a lower bound on agreement, not a measure of stale Dove entries.
